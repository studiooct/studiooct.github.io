Hiring and choice probabilities

As of: November 7, 2026

This page shows the probabilities for choices on which you can spend Diamonds obtained by paying, or in-game cash. In-game cash can also be obtained through the Starter pack or by using Diamonds, so those choices are listed too. The cash, revenue and customer numbers in the tables are in-game values, not real money or real investment returns.

1. Hiring rarity

Open hiring, Career hiring and Headhunting let you draw 1 or 10 candidates, and each candidate's rarity is drawn separately with the probabilities below. A draw of 1 costs at least 1,000,000 in-game cash for Open hiring, at least 4,000,000 in-game cash for Career hiring and 100 Diamonds for Headhunting. Cash costs rise as company sales grow and are shown on screen before you draw. A draw of 10 costs 10 times a draw of 1 (one draw of 1 in the first Headhunting guide is free). There is no pity or cumulative probability adjustment through which earlier results change the probabilities for the next candidate.

“Today's employee” is one candidate created when the date changes, using the same rarity table as Headhunting and the department probabilities below. When you hire them with Diamonds, you get exactly the employee shown on screen; there is no new draw.

* Executives can only be brought in from recruitment offers that arrive as events. You hire exactly the person shown in the offer, and their rarity is fixed at SSS.

2. Candidate department, abilities and name

Department is chosen separately from rarity. Only unlocked departments are included: Development, Marketing and Sales each have a weight of 2, and Accounting and HR each have a weight of 1; the probabilities are calculated from the total.

  • Starting level 1 · Loyalty 70%. Abilities and salary are set by rank and rarity; no extra abilities or traits are drawn separately.
  • Name: if both the common list and that department's list have unused names, each list is chosen with a 50% probability. If the chosen list has N unused names, each name has a 1/N probability. When one list is used up, names are picked from the other list; when both are used up, the full lists are used again. Which names are eligible depends on the names of your company's employees and of saved candidates.

3. Appearance

Appearance is set from a random identifier created for each new employee and does not affect abilities or salary. The probabilities below apply to every candidate created by a Headhunting, Open hiring or Career hiring draw. Each item is worked out from that identifier turned into a number, so actual results come out almost exactly at these rates (checked by running the same calculation on 2 million random identifiers). 4. Event choices that use in-game cash

These are the success and failure probabilities and results of each choice once its event has appeared. A choice that costs cash can only be picked if you have at least that much cash (except “Clarify the SLA and support hours”). How often events appear is described in section 8. The cash in the table is the smallest amount. As your company’s weekly revenue grows it rises in the same proportion, and the actual amount is shown on screen before you choose.

“All-product customers” means every released product that has customers gains or loses that share of its current customers, rounded (at least 1 per product). One choice never moves customers by more than ±10%. “Revenue xN” runs in real time (3 days = 72 hours). If a larger revenue multiplier is already running, the new one is not applied; if the new one is equal or larger, it replaces the multiplier and the later of the two end times is kept. Some choices cost money even when they fail, so please check before you choose.

5. Coffee chat

A coffee chat costs 50,000 in-game cash. The story where you drink coffee together appears 95% of the time, and the story where a little coffee gets spilled 5% of the time. Both stories have the same effect: Loyalty +5%p (up to 100%). The Loyalty increase happens 100% of the time.

6. Promotion

Product promotion such as SNS promotion and Search ads, and Company PR, have guaranteed effects (success 100% · failure 0%). Overseas promotion is decided when it finishes, and the cash paid at the start is not returned if it fails. On success the product expands into the next market; on failure it stays in its current market.

7. Fixed rewards and effects

The rewards of Diamond packs, the Daily diamond pass and the Starter pack are fixed. Diamond training, Finish now, Product slot, Resignation defense, Grant, guaranteed Diamond investment, Today's employee and executive hiring are also not ways of buying random rewards. Ad rewards and goal chests are fixed rewards too, and no random boxes are sold. One-on-ones are free, and the result depends on the answer you choose in the conversation, not on a probability.

8. Random elements that cost nothing

These random elements happen without spending cash or Diamonds.

  • Events: when a weekly settlement ends, if the company’s next goal is an office move, an investment round or an IPO and it has reached at least 70% of that goal’s amount, an event arrives with a 60% probability. No event arrives in other weeks. It is picked from the events whose conditions are met, and normal events are 3 times as likely to be picked as urgent events (weight 1 : 1/3). The same event comes at least 2 weeks apart (or its own waiting period, if longer), and urgent events come at most once every 4 weeks.
  • Bright idea customers: while work is running and you are looking at the Home screen, a bright idea appears above an employee on one of the visible floors every 40–70 seconds (evenly spread within that range) and stays for 6 seconds (not during the first guided work). Tapping it adds the customers that product would gain in 2 minutes at its current pace (at least 450).
  • Leaving investment to Accounting: it costs nothing and succeeds with 60% (Intern), 75% (Junior), 90% (Senior and Lead) or 99% (CFO), based on the rank of the strongest member of the Accounting team. Within the same rank, higher rarity moves the chance a little toward the next rank's value (for example, an SSS Intern: 71.25%). After a failure, you can hand it over again once the next weekly work has ended. Confirming with 150 Diamonds always succeeds.
