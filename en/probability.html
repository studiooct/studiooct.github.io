Hiring and choice probabilities

As of: October 21, 2026 · Draft for review before publication

This page shows the probabilities for choices on which you can spend Diamonds obtained by paying, or in-game cash. The Cash, Revenue and Brand values in the tables are in-game numbers, not real money or real investment returns.

We describe how appearance is generated, but how much per-appearance data to disclose is still under review. This page is not a determination that all legal disclosure requirements have been met.

1. Hiring rarity

The base cost to refresh Headhunting candidates is 100 Diamonds. Rarity is drawn when each candidate is created, and the same probabilities below apply to every Headhunting candidate. This draw has no pity or cumulative probability adjustment through which earlier results change the probabilities for the next candidate. Refreshes that use Diamonds use the table below and are separate from the department-guarantee exception of the first free guided hire.

Rarity probabilities for 1 candidate · Each row totals 100%
Hiring routeCBASSSSSS
Open hiring55%28%12%4%0.9%0.1%
Career hiring30%32%22%11%4.5%0.5%
Headhunting · Today's employee generation10%20%30%28%10%2%
Executive hiring*0%0%0%0%0%100%

When the date changes and a candidate is created, “Today's employee” uses the same rarity table as Headhunting. When you buy, you get exactly the employee shown on screen; there is no new draw. Open hiring and Career hiring also use the probabilities in the table when candidates are created.

* Executives are fixed at SSS in that hiring route. This does not present Executive hiring, which is still in preparation on the current release screens, as an item that is already available.

2. Candidate department, abilities, name and appearance

Department is chosen separately from rarity. Only unlocked departments are included: Development, Marketing and Sales each have a weight of 2, and Accounting and HR each have a weight of 1; the probabilities are calculated from the total.

As an exception, the Headhunting candidates in the first free guided hire show all of Development, Marketing and Sales. In some later free guided hires, the first Open hiring candidate may also be guaranteed to be in a needed department. These are separate from the weighted draw of paid Headhunting refreshes.

  • Starting level 1 · Loyalty 70%. Abilities and salary are set by rank and rarity; no extra abilities or traits are drawn separately.
  • Name: if both the common list and that department's list have unused names, each list is chosen with a 50% probability. If the chosen list has N unused names, each name has a 1/N probability. When one list is used up, names are picked from the other list; when both are used up, the full lists are used again. Which names are eligible depends on the names of your company's employees and of saved candidates.
  • Appearance: the look and colors are set from the new employee's identifier and do not affect abilities. Because the code does not guarantee independent, equal probabilities for each appearance, we have not arbitrarily listed the same probability for every appearance. How much per-appearance data to disclose will be reviewed further before publication.

4. Coffee chat

The story where you drink coffee together appears 95% of the time, and the story where a little coffee gets spilled 5% of the time. Both stories have the same effect: Loyalty +5%p (up to 100%). The Loyalty increase happens 100% of the time.

5. Promotion

The 3 promotion channels for current products and company promotion have guaranteed effects (success 100% · failure 0%). Overseas promotion and the legacy promotion routes below use separate probabilities, and their start cost is spent even if they fail.

6. Fixed rewards and effects

The rewards of Diamond packs, the Daily diamond pass and the Starter pack are fixed. Diamond training, Instant complete, Product slot, Resignation defense, Grant and guaranteed Diamond investment are also not ways of buying random rewards. Mission chests give fixed rewards, and no random boxes for purchase have been identified.
