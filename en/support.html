Support

For questions, bugs or payment issues while playing, please email us at the address below.

Email: contact.studiooct@gmail.com

When you write, please include your device name, the game version (at the bottom of the Options screen) and what happened. That helps us help you faster.

Payments and refunds

You can withdraw from a purchase within 7 days if you have not used the items.

You cannot withdraw if you have used the diamonds or cash received, or if the item takes effect immediately upon purchase.

If a minor makes a purchase without a parent’s or legal guardian’s consent, the minor or their legal guardian can cancel the purchase.

Payments are processed by Google Play. To request a refund or cancellation, use your Google Play order history or go to Options > Info > Contact support.

Moving your progress

If you sign in with the same Google Play Games account, you can pick up your progress on a new device or after reinstalling the game.
