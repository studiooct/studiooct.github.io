Grow My Startup

Information as of October 31, 2026

About the game

Grow a company one in-game week at a time with your secretary cat in this idle business game. Hire staff and assign development, marketing and sales teams to your products. Each product has its own floor: send the CEO there and hand out coffee to help the team, then improve products and expand into overseas markets.
