FAQ

Grow My Startup

Information as of October 31, 2026

What kind of game is it?

A company management game where you grow a business week by week with a secretary cat. Hire staff, develop products and watch your teams work in a building with one floor per product.

How does a weekly turn work?

Choose a development team and a promotion team for each product, then tap Start work to run one in-game week. Send the CEO to a floor or hand out coffee to help while teams work, then check the results. You do not wait a real week for each turn.

Do I earn money while away?

A share of your income builds up in the Offline vault while you are away, for up to 24 hours. The share depends on your accounting team. Collect it when you return. The income rate on screen is labelled Income/sec, with amounts shown per second (/s).

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Japanese and Traditional Chinese. The game names are 스타트업 키우기, Grow My Startup, こつこつ社長 and 小公司大老闆, respectively.

Which platforms is it coming to?

The current version is for Android. This website does not announce an iOS or PC release.

Is it free? Are purchases or ads required?

It is free to play with optional in-app purchases. You can keep playing without buying Diamonds. Rewarded ads only play when you choose them, and you can continue without watching.

How is progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device. Connecting Google Play Games also saves it automatically to Google cloud storage, so you can continue with the same account. If device and cloud progress differ, you choose which save to continue.

Is it out yet?

Now available on Google Play.
