Kitty Kingpin: Idle Alley Cat (냥아치 키우기) Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 (announced November 18, 2026)

This is a translation. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

What changed (announced November 18, 2026; effective November 25, 2026): We added coupon usage records (Member ID, coupon code and time of use) to section 3.

We added Apple Search Ads attribution information to section 4 and added ad performance analysis as a purpose for using gameplay records.

We updated the leaderboard record list in section 3 to match what is actually uploaded, added information on friends, gifts, quick messages, rivals, nickname reports and friend notifications, and removed the unused bottom-of-screen banner ad from the summary and section 5.

We added a hashed device identifier to the information listed as being sent in section 4 (announced November 19, 2026; effective starting with version 1.2.3).

Kitty Kingpin: Idle Alley Cat (Korean title: 냥아치 키우기) is a game made and operated by Studio Oct. This policy explains what information the game stores, where it is stored and who it is sent to, and how you can delete it. Contact us at contact.studiooct@gmail.com.

The policy for Grow My Startup is here.

At a glance

  • No account registration is required. Game progress is saved on your device, and also in Google Play Games (Android, if you sign in) or iCloud (iPhone).
  • For the leaderboards, coupons, friend features and to improve the game, your nickname, leaderboard records, coupon records, friend feature records and gameplay events, tied to an anonymous ID, are stored on a Google Firebase server used by Studio Oct and located in Seoul, South Korea. Your name, contact details and advertising ID are not sent.
  • The game shows Google AdMob rewarded ads, which play only when you tap to watch. Google processes information such as your advertising ID to serve these ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by the App Store and Google Play. Studio Oct does not receive your card details.
  • You can request deletion by email as described in section 9.

1. No Account Registration; Data Stored on Your Device

You can play without creating an account. Your progress (stage, currencies, gear, crew, and so on), your in-game nickname, settings such as sound and language, a record of the items you purchased, anonymous sign-in data used for our server, and gameplay events not yet sent (section 4) are stored in the app's storage on your device.

This data is used to let you continue your game and keep your settings and purchases. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your device settings. We recommend that you do not include personal information, such as your real name or contact details, in your nickname.

2. Cloud Saving — Google Play Games (Android) and iCloud (iPhone)

Android: Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, your entire game progress (including your nickname) is saved to your Google account's storage (Saved Games) and can be loaded on a new device or after reinstalling the app. During sign-in, Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID. Studio Oct does not send this information to its own servers. You can delete this game's data in the Play Games app under Settings > Your data > Delete Play Games account & data.

iPhone: Your entire game progress (including your nickname) is saved in the iCloud key-value store associated with the Apple Account signed in on your device. It can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling the app. No separate sign-in screen is required. If you do not use iCloud on your device, your progress is not saved there. Apple stores this data; Studio Oct does not send it to its own servers.

The data is kept until you delete this game's data from Google or Apple.

3. Leaderboards, Coupons and Friends — Google Firebase

The leaderboards (stage progress and weekly raid) run on Google Firebase, which is used by Studio Oct. When you start the game, it obtains an anonymous ID from Google Firebase and stores it on your device. This ID is not linked to your Google account, Apple Account or Google Play Games account.

Information stored: the anonymous ID; your nickname; the stage you have reached (and whether it is a Taiwan Chapter record); your best weekly raid score and your level at the time the score was achieved; the name of the portrait picture you chose; the country or region set on your device (a two-letter code, not location data); your title; the gear you have equipped and the skills in your quick slots; your combat power; a server-generated timestamp; and the rank and time at which you claimed a weekly reward. This information is uploaded automatically as you play.

Visibility: Other players can see your nickname, portrait, country or region, title, stage, score, and rank on their leaderboard screens, and your gear, skills and combat power when they tap your row. The anonymous ID is not displayed on screen, but it is included in the leaderboard data received by other devices.

Retention: There is no automatic deletion period. Records are deleted upon request (see section 9). There is currently no setting that turns off leaderboard uploads.

Coupon records: When you enter a coupon code in the game or on our website's coupon page, we store your Member ID (the anonymous ID above), the coupon code, and a server-generated time of use. This is used only to deliver the coupon reward to your in-game Mailbox and to prevent the same coupon from being used twice. Only the game on your device can read it, and it is not shown to other players. There is no automatic deletion period; records are deleted upon request (see section 9).

Friends: We store your list of mutually added friends (each friend's anonymous ID and the time they were added). Only you can see your friend list.

Gifts: When you send a gift to a friend, we store the sender's anonymous ID and the date and time. This is used to limit gifts to one per day and to deliver them.

Quick messages: When you send a friend one of six preset messages, we store the number of the message you chose, the sender's anonymous ID, and the date and time. It is deleted once the recipient reads it.

Rivals (from update 1.2.1): We store rival requests and your current rival (their anonymous ID and the time). These are deleted when a request is declined or canceled, or when the rivalry is ended.

Nickname reports: When you report another player's nickname, we store the anonymous IDs of the reporter and the reported player, the reported nickname, the reason number you chose, and the time. Only Studio Oct can see reports, and they are used only to block inappropriate nicknames. A blocked nickname is stored with that player's anonymous ID and cannot appear on the leaderboards.

Friend notifications: If you turn on friend notifications in Settings and allow notifications on your device, we store your device's notification token, device type (iOS or Android), game language and time zone so that friends' quick messages can be sent to you as notifications. The time zone is used to avoid sending notifications at night. This is deleted when you turn notifications off.

Retention: Unless stated otherwise above, there is no automatic deletion period; records are deleted upon request (see section 9).

4. Gameplay Events — Used to Improve the Game and Measure Our Ads

To understand where players get stuck or stop playing and improve the game, the game sends gameplay events to a Google Firebase server used by Studio Oct in Seoul, South Korea. These events are linked to the same anonymous ID described in section 3. Events are collected on your device and sent in batches every few minutes. Events that cannot be sent remain on your device and are sent later.

Information sent: session start and end (session length), stage progress and combat power, amounts of in-game currency, boss battle results (win or loss, time taken, number of attempts), tutorial steps and skips, screens (tabs) opened, number of draws, crew recruitment, the fact that a purchase or ad view occurred (item name or ad placement), app version, device type (iOS or Android), game language, number of days since installation, and server-generated timestamps.

Device identifier: To avoid counting the same device more than once, starting with version 1.2.3, the game also sends a hash of the device identifier provided to the game (identifierForVendor on iPhone, ANDROID_ID on Android). The original identifier cannot be recovered from the hash. This is not an advertising ID and is not used for advertising or tracking. The hash may change if you uninstall and reinstall the app.

Apple Search Ads attribution: whether the install came through an Apple Search Ads ad and, if so, that ad's campaign, ad group and keyword IDs and the App Store country or region where the ad ran (not your device's location). This is received through Apple's AdServices framework on the device and used only to measure whether our ads led to installs. No third-party SDK or advertising ID is used, it does not depend on tracking permission, and it contains no name or contact details.

Information not sent: your name, contact details, email address, advertising ID, precise location or payment card details.

Only Studio Oct can see these records. They are not shown to other players or made public, and are used only to improve the game and to measure the effectiveness of our ads. They are not used to select which ads to show.

Retention: There is no automatic deletion period. Records are deleted when the game service ends or upon request (see section 9). There is currently no setting that turns off sending gameplay events.

5. Advertising — Google AdMob

Rewarded ads (ads you watch to receive a reward) play only when you tap to watch one. The game has no banner ads that stay on screen and no full-screen interstitial ads. Ads are served by AdMob, a service of Google LLC. You can keep playing without watching ads.

To serve and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), your IP address and the approximate location estimated from it, records of ads you viewed and tapped, app and device information, and diagnostic data such as errors and performance. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

On iPhone, the game asks whether you want to allow tracking the first time you launch it. If you do not allow tracking, your IDFA is not shared with Google and ads will not be personalized using your IDFA. Your gameplay and rewards remain the same either way. You can change this setting later under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID to turn off personalized ads. In Android Settings, go to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

If you buy the "Remove Ads" item, you receive rewarded-ad rewards without watching ads.

6. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Studio Oct does not receive payment information such as your name or card number.

The game receives the purchase result from the store (item and transaction ID) to deliver the item, and saves a record of the purchase on your device. The fact that a purchase occurred and the item name are also included in the gameplay events in section 4.

Refunds follow each store's refund policy and process.

7. Other Information

Studio Oct does not collect your name, contact details, precise location, or other information on your device such as photos or contacts.

The game engine (Unity) may send anonymous device statistics, such as your device model and operating system, to Unity Technologies. This information does not include your name, nickname, or game progress.

8. Third-Party Services and International Transfers

Studio Oct does not operate its own account server. Information is sent to the following services over the internet while you play, for the features described above.

Google LLC — Firebase Anonymous Authentication: Issues the anonymous ID. Information may be processed in Google's data centers in the United States and other countries.

Google LLC — Firebase storage (Cloud Firestore): Stores the leaderboard, coupon and friend feature records in section 3 and the gameplay events in section 4 in a data center in Seoul, Republic of Korea.

Google LLC — Firebase Cloud Messaging; Apple Inc. — Apple Push Notification service (iPhone): Delivery of the friend notifications described in section 3. Notification data may be processed at Google and Apple data centers in the United States and other countries.

Google LLC — Google Play Games: Processes the player ID and progress data in section 2 (Android). Information may be processed in Google's data centers in the United States and other countries.

Google LLC — AdMob: Processes the advertising information in section 5 in Google's data centers in the United States and other countries; retention follows Google's policies.

Apple Inc. — iCloud: the progress data in section 2 (iPhone); App Store payments: the purchases in section 6. Information may be processed in Apple's data centers in the United States and other countries.

Google LLC — Google Play billing: the purchases in section 6 (Android). Information may be processed in Google's data centers in the United States and other countries.

Unity Technologies — Processes the anonymous device statistics in section 7. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries.

If you do not want your data transferred internationally, you can choose not to sign in to Google Play Games, turn off iCloud, or decline tracking permission on iPhone. To request deletion of your leaderboard records and gameplay events, email the address provided in section 9.

9. Requests for Access, Correction, Deletion, or Restriction of Processing

You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing at contact.studiooct@gmail.com. Please put "Kitty Kingpin privacy request" in the subject and describe your request in the body. Leaderboard records and gameplay events carry no name or account, so we may ask for minimal details such as your nickname and stage in order to find your records. We will tell you the outcome within 10 days of receiving your request.

Support email: Emails, messages, and attachments you send to us are stored in Gmail and used to respond to your inquiries. They are deleted three years after the matter is resolved.

In Korea, you may also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972) for consultation or to file a complaint.

10. Children's Privacy

Kitty Kingpin: Idle Alley Cat is not directed at children under the age of 14. If we become aware that information relating to a child under the age of 14 has been stored, we will delete it without delay.
