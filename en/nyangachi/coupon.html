Redeem a coupon

Enter a coupon code and your reward will be sent to the game's Mailbox.

How to find your Member ID

  1. In the game, open Settings and tap "Coupon". On iPhone, tap "Member ID" instead.
  2. Tap "Copy" next to your Member ID and paste it into the field above.
  • Each coupon can be used once per Member ID.
  • If you enter someone else's ID, the reward goes to them.
  • On Android, you can also enter codes in the game under Settings → Coupon.
