What the game is like

01

Plan the week, press one button

Assign a development team and a promotion team to each app you're building, then press Start work and one in-game week goes by. You manage new apps, updates and promotion on one screen. As the CEO, you also pick your own task for the week: sales, a talk, an investor meeting or a rest.

02

Hire your team, look after them

Bring in Today's employee to build your team, and train them to grow their skills. When someone can't focus on work, talk it over in a One-on-one. How they feel depends on the answer you pick.

03

A Weekly report at the end of each week

When the week ends, the Weekly report shows your revenue, spending and app development progress at a glance. Even an app that starts small moves up in grade as you keep updating it, and its customers and revenue grow too.

It starts in a small office.
Next stop: the global market

Your company grows slowly but steadily. How far you take it is up to you.

  1. Step 1

    Start in a tiny one-room office

    One employee, a few desks. Build your first app and earn your first revenue.

  2. Step 2

    Land an investment

    As revenue builds up, investment chances come along. When you get investment, your Company tier goes up and you can build more apps at the same time.

  3. Step 3

    To Asia, then the world

    As customers gather, expand overseas to Asia, the Americas & Europe, and then the whole world. When your team grows, move to a bigger office.

One week at a time, whenever you like

Busy? Open it for a moment and play one week. Got time? Play as long as you like. There's no bankruptcy and no game over. Your secretary cat keeps track of news and urgent matters for you.
