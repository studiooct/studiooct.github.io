Recruiting…

A day at your company

Shh! Someone's napping at the office

Someone’s dozing off? Tap the floor and the CEO rides the elevator over.

  1. Dozing? Go wake them up!

    Teams without a lead nod off when you’re away, and their bars stop too.

  2. Tap a floor and off you go

    You ride the elevator over. Sleepy staff wake right up and the floor runs twice as fast.

  3. Pass around some coffee

    A coffee makes that floor 2.5 times as fast for a moment, and 3.5 times with you there.

Every full bar pops out customers and cash!

Hiring

Meet your next team member

Pick a hiring channel and tap draw. The cat scrolls through the monitor, résumés flip over one by one and a grade stamp lands on each. High grades sparkle!

You don’t have to hire all ten. Just tap the people you like.

Apps

Grow small products from home to the world

Pick an icon and a colour, give it a name, and it’s your company’s product.

Stuck? Tap suggest for names like Sleepy Diary or Cozy Cafe.

After launch, update it, promote it and sell it to bring in the money.

Events

Offices are full of surprises

What would you do? Give one a tap!

A manager pressures staff into overtime

Manager Park is lecturing the team about grit and making everyone stay late.

An office romance

Rumor has it that two teammates are dating.

A CEO post goes wrong

A screenshot of your social post is spreading, and cancellation requests are starting to come in.

There are more than twenty surprises like these.

Weekly work

Watch your company grow every week

Put development, promotion and sales teams on each app, tap start, and your company runs for three minutes. Then your weekly report arrives, with this week’s cash and each app’s results on one page.

Staff care

Your team has feelings too

If someone’s thinking about quitting, sit down for a one-on-one. You can give a bonus or have a coffee chat too.

Send people to training and interns grow into juniors, then seniors.

CEO life

Your success shows on you too

When your company earns, your own savings grow.

Buy new clothes, move house, change your car, and your CEO profile changes with them.

Secretary

Your secretary cat keeps you posted

Weekly reports and urgent news all come to the secretary room. You can see customers, your next funding goal and your headcount in one place.
