“Grow My Startup” Privacy Policy

This is a translation. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version applies.

Effective date: October 23, 2026

“Grow My Startup” is a game made and operated by Studio Oct (스튜디오옥트), a sole proprietorship registered in Korea. This policy explains what information the game stores, where it stores it and to whom it sends it, and how you can turn that off or delete it. Please send any questions to contact.studiooct@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • There is no account sign-up. Your progress is saved on your device and, if you connect Google Play Games, is also saved automatically to Google's storage.
  • Usage data for improving the game and crash reports are on by default. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, they are sent only if you consent. You can turn them off at any time in Options → Info tab → “Send usage data to improve the game”.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play, and Studio Oct does not receive payment method information such as card numbers.
  • The only ads are rewarded ads that you choose to watch yourself.
  • You can request deletion or a stop to processing by email, as described in Section 8.

1. Sign-up and storage on your device

You can play the game without signing up for an account. The following information needed for the game is kept in the app storage on your device.

  • Text you set yourself in the game, such as company, CEO and employee names
  • Progress (employees, products, in-game currency, week number, etc.), records of rewards and purchases delivered, and copies for save recovery
  • Settings such as sound, language, notifications, ranking participation and sending usage data, and a random identifier created on the device

Purpose: continuing your game, keeping your settings, preventing rewards and purchases from being delivered twice, and recovering saves. We recommend that you do not enter real personal information in name fields.

Retention and deletion: kept until you uninstall the app or delete the app's data in Android settings. If you use Android device backup, this information may be included in that backup. Deleting the data on your device does not delete records saved with Google (Section 4) or usage data and crash reports already sent (Sections 2 and 3).

Notifications: If you turn on notifications, scheduled notifications are sent from within your device (no information is sent to a server). Only game progress notifications, such as work being finished, are sent; promotional notifications are not sent. The notification feature is added through an update, and on Android 13 or later you are asked once for notification permission. You can turn notifications off at any time in Options → Game tab or in Android settings.

2. Usage analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

Outside the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, sending usage data to improve the game is on from the first launch. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, data is sent only if you allow it on Google's consent screen (UMP). If your region or consent status cannot be confirmed, nothing is sent.

  • Information the game sends: use of screens, tabs and pop-ups and the time spent on them; predefined codes and results of in-game actions such as hiring, training, promotion, products, investment, missions, settings and watching ads; company stage, game week, in-game money range, employee count range, number of products, markets and office stage; app version; and time of occurrence.
  • Information Google's tools (SDK) process automatically: the app instance ID that distinguishes app installations; app, device, operating system and language information; basic usage information such as app launches and updates; and approximate location estimated from the IP address. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses themselves. Information such as in-app purchases (item, price, currency) and ad impressions may also be recorded automatically (Google's guide to automatically collected events).
  • Information not sent: names, email, text entered in the game, exact amounts of in-game money, Google account, Play Games ID, full save contents, receipts and payment tokens. Analytics' advertising ID collection and ad personalization features are turned off. Processing of information for ads is explained in Section 6.
  • Purpose: to see which content players enjoy and where they run into difficulty, and to improve the game and its stability. Studio Oct reviews the results in the Firebase console.
  • Retention period: user-level and event-level data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregate statistics that cannot identify individuals may remain separately from this period (Google's guide to data retention).

Turning it off: turning off Options → Info tab → “Send usage data to improve the game” stops both usage analytics and crash reporting (Section 3). The off setting is saved on your device right away and stays in effect when you restart the game, and turning it off causes no disadvantage in playing, saving or rewards. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, to turn it back on you must also allow it on the consent screen.

Deletion: turning it off does not by itself delete records already sent. If you want them deleted, please request it by email as described in Section 8.

3. Crash reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

Crash reporting follows the “Send usage data to improve the game” setting in Section 2. When it is on and the game suddenly closes or an error occurs, a report is created and sent to Google Firebase the next time the game is launched. Reports are sent only when the setting is on for both the session in which the error occurred and the next session; if you turn it on during a session, it applies from the next launch.

  • Information sent: the type of error and the code location where it occurred; the time it occurred and the app state; environment information such as app version, operating system and device model; Crashlytics installation UUID, Firebase installation ID and session identifier; and game usage records from just before the error (the information in Section 2).
  • Information not sent: names, email, text you entered, payment tokens, save contents, and any separately assigned user ID. For errors that arise in the game's code, the message text and file paths are removed before sending. However, because device environment information and identifiers are included, these reports cannot be called fully anonymous information.
  • Purpose: to identify the causes of errors and the number of affected users, and to fix them.
  • Retention period: according to Google, crash records and related identifiers are kept for 90 days, after which the deletion process from Google's systems begins (Firebase privacy information).

Turning off and deletion: turn it off with the same switch as in Section 2. To have reports that were already sent deleted, please request it by email as described in Section 8.

4. Google Play Games connection, auto-save and rankings

Connecting Google Play Games is optional. Without connecting, you can still play with your progress saved on this device. If your device has a Play Games profile, the game may connect automatically when it starts. When connected, the game uses your Play Games player ID and profile nickname. Neither the game nor Studio Oct receives your Google account password.

  • Auto-save: your entire game progress is automatically saved to the Google storage (Saved Games) of the connected account, and loaded on a new device or after you reinstall the game. This includes in-game names (company, CEO, employees); employee, product, currency and progress records; purchase delivery records (including hashes of transaction confirmation values); save time, play time, device model and save file identifier; and the previous save.
  • Rankings: each time an in-game week (a sprint) ends and its results are saved, scores are posted to Play Games leaderboards. The scores are cumulative revenue, revenue per employee, and the "This week's app ranking" score (for each app, customers brought in by sprints divided by that app's market size, added up). The "This week's app ranking" score carries a short tag with your main app's icon number and color number, the number of the default app name chosen by the Game, and how many apps you have. Company valuation rankings are posted only after a rule for converting company valuation into a score has been applied in the game. On leaderboards, your Play Games nickname, score and rank, and the icon and default app name in the tag, are visible to other players. Your company name, app names you wrote yourself and save contents are not made public. "This week's app ranking" restarts every Sunday at 07:00 UTC.
  • Purpose: continuing play after changing devices, save recovery, and comparing rankings.
  • Retention period: kept until you delete this game's data from Google. The game sets no separate expiry period, and the time Google takes to process deletion follows the Google retention policy.

Turning off: for rankings, if you turn off Options → Account tab → “Share my ranking” (on by default) and save, no new records are posted after that. Scores already posted are not deleted just by turning it off. If you do not want saves stored with Google, play without a Play Games profile, or turn off automatic sign-in to games in your Play Games settings (menus may differ by device and version).

Deletion: your progress saved with Google and this game's Play Games data (including leaderboard scores) can be deleted by choosing this game under Play Games profile → My data → Delete Play Games account & data → Delete individual game data. In the Play Store app, you can find this under profile icon → Settings → General → Play Games profile → Privacy & settings (menu names may vary by version; Google's guide). Deleting your Google data does not delete the save on your device, so you need to delete that separately, and if you connect again and play, new records will be saved.

Name reports: When other players' names appear in the rankings, you can report an inappropriate name. A report stores the reported player's Play Games player ID, display name, score tag, week, reason and time, together with an anonymous ID that Google Firebase creates for the reporting device, in Google Firebase Realtime Database (Singapore). Your own name or account is not sent. Studio Oct reviews reports within 7 days and may hide the name; the list of hidden player IDs is public so the game can read it, and an ID is removed when its name is unhidden. Report records are deleted 1 year after they are received.

5. In-app purchases — Google Play billing

Paid items such as Diamond packs and passes are purchased through Google Play billing. The game communicates with Google Play through Unity's payment integration tool (Unity IAP). Payment method information such as card and bank account numbers is processed directly by Google; neither the game nor Studio Oct receives it.

  • Information processed: product ID, order (transaction) identifier, purchase status, receipt and purchase token. The game verifies these on the device and then delivers the item.
  • Storage: the confirmation values (hashes) of delivered transactions, whether you own the Starter pack, and pass claim records are kept in device storage and, if you have connected Play Games, are also included in the Google save (Section 4).
  • Purpose: delivering and restoring items, and preventing duplicate delivery.
  • Purchase records sent by Unity IAP: when a purchase is attempted, succeeds, fails or is delivered, the payment tool also sends to Unity, encrypted, the item, price, currency, transaction identifier, Google Play receipt, time of occurrence, app, operating system and device environment, install time, and the install and session identifiers available. Your IP address and approximate location may be processed while connecting to the server. The purpose is to provide the payment service and to analyze the purchase flow and performance. This transmission is separate from the usage data setting in Section 2 and may take place during purchases even if that setting is turned off.
  • Retention period: purchase records in device and Google saves are kept for as long as those saves are kept. Google's order, payment and refund records are kept in accordance with Google's policies and applicable laws, and remain even if you delete your game save.

Purchases are optional, and you can play without buying anything. Please manage orders, refunds and payment methods in your Google Play order history and Google account (Google Play Help Center). Unity's description of its payment integration tool is in Unity IAP privacy information.

6. Rewarded ads — Google AdMob and UMP

The game has only rewarded ads, and an ad plays only when you choose to watch it. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and no separate ad mediation tool is used. Even if you do not watch ads, when the game runs, AdMob and Google's consent tool (UMP) start up to check consent status and prepare ads, and the following information may be processed at that time.

  • Information: IP address and approximate location estimated from it; device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID (which may include identifiers related to the account signed in on the device); device and app information; usage records such as app launches, taps and video views; performance and diagnostic information for the app and ad tools; and your consent choices.
  • Purpose: serving ads and measuring their performance, applying your privacy choices, and preventing fraud. This information may be processed by Google and Google's advertising partners (Google's explanation of how it uses information from partner apps).
  • Retention period: follows the Google retention policy, depending on the type of information and the purpose.

Choices and changes: in regions where consent is required, Google's consent screen appears, and afterwards you can choose again at any time in Options → Info tab → “Ad privacy settings” (this item appears only in regions where consent is required). In all regions, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in the ads menu of Android settings, and you can turn off personalized ads in Google My Ad Center. Even if you turn off personalized ads, non-personalized ads and the processing needed for measurement and fraud prevention may continue.

7. External services and international transfers

Studio Oct does not operate its own game servers or account servers (only name reports are stored in Google Firebase). For the features above, information is transferred to Google LLC and Unity Technologies. For usage analytics, crash reports and support emails, Studio Oct has entrusted processing to Google; Play Games, payments, ads and Unity's processing of payment records follow each service's own policies. Transfers take place only to the extent necessary for the purposes stated in each section above.

8. Requests for access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing

You can request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing by emailing contact.studiooct@gmail.com. Please write “Grow My Startup privacy request” in the subject line, and in the body describe your request and what it concerns (device save, Google save, rankings, usage analytics, crash reports, ads, support emails). Do not send passwords, card numbers or resident registration (national ID) numbers. A guardian (legal representative) or an authorized person may also make a request in the same way.

Because no name or account is attached to usage analytics and crash reports, we may ask for a minimum of additional information, such as the app version and period of use, to find your records. We will reply within 10 days at the latest after receiving a request. Records we can find will be deleted or their processing stopped, and we will tell you the result; for data that cannot be identified or that must be kept by law, we will tell you the reason. Data tied to your Google account (Google save, rankings, ad settings) can be deleted directly through the Google menus in Sections 4 and 6, and we will explain how if needed.

Support emails: if you contact us by email, the email address, message body and attachments you send are kept in Gmail and used to reply to you. They are deleted 3 years after the date handling is completed, and even before then, if you ask, we will delete them to the extent there is no legal obligation to keep them. Please do not attach sensitive material.

Access to, deletion of and other requests regarding payment records processed by Unity can be made to unity-iap-contact@unity3d.com. If needed, please ask Studio Oct how to do this. Deleting your device save or turning off usage data for improving the game does not automatically delete purchase records already sent to Unity.

Destruction and security measures: data whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been fulfilled is deleted without delay in a way that cannot be recovered. We do not create paper documents. Information the game sends is transmitted encrypted, and the analytics and crash results and the support mailbox are used only by the operator personally.

You may also seek advice on or report a privacy infringement to the following agencies (Korea): Personal Information Infringement Report Center (dial 118 without an area code), Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972), Supreme Prosecutors' Office (dial 1301 without an area code), Korean National Police Agency (dial 182 without an area code).

9. Children and teenagers

“Grow My Startup” is not directed at children under the age of 14. We do not have separate child-directed ad settings or a parental consent process. If you learn that information of a child under the age of 14 has been processed, please let us know at the email address above. We will explain how to turn off sending usage data and will delete the data we can find.
